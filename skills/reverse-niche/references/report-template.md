# <Niche or @channel>: niche report (<scan date>)

## 1. Numbers
- Channels scanned, with videos, total views, median, cadence per day and age.
- Top 10 table: title · views · ×median · length.
- Outliers and flops: counts, and what share of total views the top video holds.
- Confidence: sample size per channel (exploratory / low / moderate / high), and whether one hit dominates (mean ÷ median).
- Fatigue: newest 10 median vs channel median and vs the previous 10, hit rate now vs before, and the verdict (growing / steady / tiring).
- Title patterns: which pattern has the best median ×median (only n ≥ 3).

## 2. Format
Theme, title pattern, hook, structure, length, technique, voice, captions, sound, language and market, CTA, series.

## 3. What works
3–6 bullets, each backed by outliers ("all 5 outliers show the matchup in frame 1"). Mark each one **recipe** (repeated hits) or **lead** (one hit).
Then list what does not carry over to a new channel: the creator's fame, a one-time moment, a collab, a peaked sound.

## 4. What flops
3–6 bullets, each backed by flops in the same format, with the difference from the outliers.

## 5. Weaknesses to beat
A numbered list. Every item gets a fix in section 6.

## 6. Our design
Name, palette and type, hook, structure with timings (beat sheet), the signature detail, CTA, and what stays recognisable.
For each weakness from section 5: weakness → fix.

## 7. Episode ideas
5–10 ideas: title · hook line · which outlier it builds on.

## 8. Sources
Channel URLs, the video IDs you watched, and the scan date.
