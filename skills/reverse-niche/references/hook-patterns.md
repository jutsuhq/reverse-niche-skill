# Hook and title patterns

Use these labels to name what an outlier does in its first 3 s and in its title, so outliers and flops can be compared.
They are practitioner patterns collected from creator tutorials, not research results: trust the channel's own numbers over this list.

## Spoken or on-screen hook types

| Type | What it does | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| Matchup | two or more sides, the viewer picks one and waits for the result | "A vs B", "$1 vs $1000" |
| Open loop | promises an answer that arrives later | "Wait for the last one", "Watch what happens at step 40" |
| Bold claim | a statement the viewer wants to check or argue with | "This is the worst way to…" |
| Problem | names a pain the viewer has right now | "If your … keeps …" |
| Benefit first | the result before the method | "This saves you 2 hours a week" |
| POV / identity | casts the viewer into a role | "POV: you're the …", "Which one are you?" |
| Number | a concrete count or price sets the stakes | "3 mindsets, 50 steps" |
| Pattern break | an image or sound that does not belong, with no words | the first frame alone stops the scroll |

Score the three layers separately: what is **seen** in frame 1, what is **read** on screen, and what is **heard**. Strong outliers usually say the same thing in all three.

## Loops

- Close the loop the hook opened. If several loops are opened, close the first one last.
- End soon after the payoff: a long tail after the answer loses the viewers who stayed.
- A loopable last frame (it leads back into the first) earns replays.

## Title patterns in `scan_channel.py`

`vs`, `number`, `question`, `pov`, `first person`, `how/why`, `list`. The scan prints the median ×median for each pattern with at least 3 videos.
A pattern that is high on one channel is a lead; high on several channels in the niche, a recipe.

## Borrowing a format

A format that works in one niche often works in a neighbouring one ("$1 vs $100", a ranking, a countdown race).
Keep the mechanism, change the subject, and add one detail of your own.
